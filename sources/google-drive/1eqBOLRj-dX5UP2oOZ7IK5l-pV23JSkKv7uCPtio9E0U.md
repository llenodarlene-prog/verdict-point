## 3\. APPROVED EVIDENCE AND CLAIM LIMITS

Evidence 1\. CONFIRMED: Activity records supported work; AutoTime creates time entries from it nightly. It must be enabled per user; entries can be reviewed and edited. AutoTime entries are not automatically billed.  
[Evidence 1 source: AutoTime Basics](https://support.smokeball.com/hc/en-us/articles/5860708227863-AutoTime-Basics)  
Evidence 2\. CONFIRMED: Missing matter selection, zero duration, ignored activity types, leads, closed matters depending on settings, and excluded pending fees can prevent time-entry creation. Editing the generated entry does not change the underlying Activity record.  
[Evidence 2 source: AutoTime Basics](https://support.smokeball.com/hc/en-us/articles/5860708227863-AutoTime-Basics)  
Evidence 3\. QUALIFIED VENDOR CLAIM: AutoTime Basics claims users bill 10–30% more. This is not an independent outcome verified here.  
[Evidence 3 source: AutoTime Basics](https://support.smokeball.com/hc/en-us/articles/5860708227863-AutoTime-Basics)  
Evidence 4\. QUALIFIED VENDOR CLAIM: Family Law Billing says AutoTime clients capture 34% more time than manual users. 'Captured time' and 'billed more' are different measures; neither establishes collected revenue or profit.  
[Evidence 4 source: Family Law Billing](https://support.smokeball.com/hc/en-us/articles/5962336205591-Family-Law-Billing)
