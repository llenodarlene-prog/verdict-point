## 3\. APPROVED EVIDENCE AND CLAIM LIMITS

Evidence 1\. CONFIRMED: Current case/annual subscription pricing is based on managed data and usage, with unlimited users/uploads. Single-document AI actions and Writing Assistant are included; batch AI actions use credits, and unused committed credits expire at term end. No universal public per-GB rate is supplied.  
[Evidence 1 source: Everlaw Pricing | Clear, Predictable Pricing, Unsurpassed Value](https://www.everlaw.com/pricing-b/)  
Evidence 2\. CONFIRMED: Billing FAQ measures peak native plus peak processed data during the month; adding the same documents to subprojects does not automatically double bill them. Productions themselves are included but storing produced files is billable. The FAQ also lists optional services; its 'entirely data based' wording must be read alongside current AI-credit pricing.  
[Evidence 2 source: Billing and Pricing FAQ](https://support.everlaw.com/hc/en-us/articles/205660765-Billing-and-Pricing-FAQ)  
Evidence 3\. CONFIRMED: Searches are private to their creator by default; shared-search permissions distinguish View, Edit and Full Access. Edits/version history are shared, whereas a personal copy does not update collaborators' search.  
[Evidence 3 source: Sharing and Collaborating on Searches](https://support.everlaw.com/hc/en-us/articles/25390790827035-Sharing-and-Collaborating-on-Searches)  
Evidence 4\. CONFIRMED SCOPE: Object sharing, project permissions and administrative/global access are separate layers. Sharing an object is not a substitute for a complete project-access review.  
[Evidence 4 source: Sharing and Object Permissions](https://support.everlaw.com/hc/en-us/articles/210011233-Sharing-and-Object-Permissions)
