Evidence 1\. CONFIRMED: The 2025 KPI page reports utilization 38% (3.0 billable hours per eight-hour day), realization 88% (2.6 invoiced hours), collection 93% (2.4 collected hours). These are published rounded figures; multiplying rounded averages need not reproduce displayed hours.  
[Evidence 1 source: Law Firm KPIs | Key Performance Indicators & Benchmarks](https://www.clio.com/resources/legal-trends/benchmarks/)  
Evidence 2\. CONFIRMED: 2025 median realization lockup is 43 days; collection lockup 32 days; total lockup 93 days. Component medians do not add to the median of their sum. Do not stack 43 and 32 as if they explain 93\.  
[Evidence 2 source: Law Firm KPIs | Key Performance Indicators & Benchmarks](https://www.clio.com/resources/legal-trends/benchmarks/)  
Evidence 3\. CONFIRMED DEFINITIONS: Firm-level realization lockup \= unbilled-work value / annual revenue × 365; collection lockup \= unpaid-invoice value / annual revenue × 365\. Total is their sum for that same firm and denominator.  
[Evidence 3 source: Law Firm KPIs | Key Performance Indicators & Benchmarks](https://www.clio.com/resources/legal-trends/benchmarks/)
