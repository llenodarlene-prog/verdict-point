Clio 2025 Legal Trends Report:

Clio 2024 client-intake research:  
https\://www\.clio.com/guides/client-intake-legal-trends/  
Third-party mystery shop of 500 firms:  
• 40% answered phone calls  
• 33% responded to email inquiries  
• 52% answered or returned a phone call, so 48% were effectively unreachable by phone  
• only 30% could easily understand the hiring process from the website  
• 14% could find pricing information  
Do not turn these into universal conversion rates.

WordStream/LocaliQ 2026 US search-advertising benchmark:  
https\://www\.wordstream.com/blog/2026-google-ads-benchmarks  
13,474 US-based search campaigns, Apr 2025–Mar 2026:  
• Attorneys & Legal Services CTR: 5.87%  
• reported benchmark CPC: \$9.87  
• reported benchmark CPL: \$131.63  
• highest reported CPC and CPL among industries in the study  
Methodology note: WordStream presents “averages” but explains the calculations are technically medians to reduce outlier effects; data spans Google and Microsoft Ads.

ABA 2024 Websites & Marketing TechReport:  
https\://www\.americanbar.org/groups/law\_practice/resources/tech-report/2024/2024-websites-and-marketing-techreport/  
ABA 2024 survey is based on practicing lawyers. Useful for channel/adoption context and firm-size differences.

LMA / Above the Law 2025 Decision-Makers Survey:  
https\://www\.legalmarketing.org/Advocacy/Industry-Research  
Nearly 100 senior marketing/BD leaders:  
• 44% reported budgets increased slightly after inflation  
• 10% increased significantly

Clio 2026 Solo & Small Firm Report:  
https\://www\.clio.com/resources/legal-trends/2026-solo-small-firm-report/  
Use only for 2026 small-firm context. The report is based on a survey of 1,700+ respondents plus anonymized usage data from tens of thousands of legal professionals.
