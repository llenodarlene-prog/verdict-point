# Personal Injury Settlement Calculator

A personal injury claim starts with the losses you can document. This calculator adds up medical bills, lost wages, and other costs, lets you test a pain and suffering figure of your own, and shows what is left after shared fault, the attorney fee, case costs, and medical liens. It does not predict a settlement.

```tool
personal-injury
```

## How the Calculator Works

1. **Economic losses:** It adds the medical bills, future care, lost wages, future earnings, property damage, and other costs you enter.
2. **Pain and suffering:** It adds the figure you choose to test. No multiplier is applied, because there is no fixed formula in the law.
3. **Shared fault:** It reduces the total by your share of fault.
4. **Policy limit:** It caps the amount at any insurance policy limit you enter.
5. **Net amount:** It subtracts the fee, costs, and liens.

## Florida's Fault Rule

Florida uses modified comparative fault. Under [section 768.81 of the 2026 Florida Statutes](https://www.flsenate.gov/Laws/Statutes/2026/768.81), your recovery is reduced by your share of fault. A party found greater than 50 percent at fault for their own harm may not recover any damages. That bar does not apply to medical negligence claims under chapter 766. Choose Florida in the calculator to apply this rule.

Florida's [section 95.11](https://www.flsenate.gov/Laws/Statutes/2026/95.11) sets a two-year limit for most actions founded on negligence.

## Knee Injury Mode

Knee injuries often lead to future care, such as physical therapy, injections, or surgery. Knee mode loads an example with future medical costs and reminds you to enter only costs a doctor has estimated.

## Worked Example

A Florida driver has a knee injury with $24,000 in medical bills, $18,000 in estimated future care, $9,500 in lost wages, and $800 in other costs. That is $52,300 in economic losses. They test a $40,000 pain and suffering figure, for a $92,300 claim. At 10% fault, the claim falls to $83,070. A 33% fee, $2,500 in costs, and $8,000 in liens come out of that.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is There a Formula for Personal Injury Settlements?

No fixed formula exists in the law. Insurers and lawyers weigh documented losses, the strength of the evidence, fault, and policy limits. This calculator lets you test figures rather than guess a multiplier.

### What if I Am Partly at Fault in Florida?

Your recovery is reduced by your share of fault. If you are found more than 50% at fault for your own harm, Florida s. 768.81(6) bars recovery, except in medical negligence claims.

### How Long Do I Have to File in Florida?

Section 95.11(5)(a) sets two years for an action founded on negligence.
