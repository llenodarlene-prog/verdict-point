# Legal Technology FAQ: Software, AI and Legal Research

Legal technology covers the software firms use to run matters, bill clients, manage documents, and research the law. These answers explain what each tool category does, how vendors price it, and where AI fits in.

Each answer links to a longer guide. If you're shortlisting tools, start with these dated comparisons:

- **[Lawyer time tracking software](/legal-tech/best-lawyer-time-tracking-software/):** passive capture, timers, and billing links.
- **[Legal billing software](/legal-tech/best-legal-billing-software/):** invoicing, LEDES, and payment fees.
