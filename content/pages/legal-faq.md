## How We Write and Check These Answers

Every answer starts with the direct response, so you don't have to scroll for it. Then we add the detail you can use: a table, a worked example, or the steps.

We hold each answer to a few rules:

- **Sources sit next to the claim.** If an answer states a rule, a number, or a definition someone could dispute, the source is linked right below it.
- **We open every source ourselves.** Each one was checked against the live page, not a search snippet or someone else's summary.
- **Examples are labeled.** When we invent figures to show the math, we say so.
- **Rules can vary.** Where a state, court, or employer may do things differently, the answer says that.

This page explains how the legal industry works in general. It isn't legal advice, and it can't tell you what to do in your own case. For that, talk to a lawyer licensed where you live.
