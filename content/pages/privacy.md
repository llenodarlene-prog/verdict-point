# Privacy Policy

**Effective date: September 1, 2026**

Verdict Point is an independent publication about the business of law. This policy explains what information is handled when you read verdictpoint.org or contact us, why it is handled, and the choices you have. We keep it short because we collect very little.

## What We Do Not Collect

The site has no user accounts, no comment system, no newsletter sign-up, and no contact forms. We do not run analytics tools, advertising networks, social media tracking pixels, or session recording. The site does not set cookies.

## Information Handled When You Visit

Like most websites, a few technical details are processed automatically when your browser requests a page.

### Server Logs at Our Hosting Provider

The site is hosted by Hostinger. Its servers record standard technical information for each request, such as your IP address, the date and time, the page requested, the referring page, and your browser type. These logs are used to deliver the site, keep it secure, and diagnose errors. We do not use them to build profiles of readers. Hostinger's handling of this data is described in its own privacy policy.

### Fonts Loaded From Google

The site's typefaces are served by Google Fonts. When a page loads, your browser connects to Google's servers, which receive your IP address and browser details. Google describes how it uses this information in the Google Fonts and Google privacy documentation.

## Information You Choose to Send Us

If you email us at [verdictpoint@gmail.com](mailto:verdictpoint@gmail.com), we receive your email address, your name if you include it, and anything you write. Our email is provided by Google through Gmail. We use these messages only to read and respond to your enquiry, such as an editorial tip, a correction request, or a contributor or business question. We do not sell or rent email addresses, and we do not add them to mailing lists.

We keep correspondence only as long as it is useful for the enquiry, for maintaining an accurate record of corrections, or for meeting legal obligations. You can ask us to delete your messages at any time.

## Links to Other Websites

Our articles link to sources such as government data, vendor documentation, and industry research. When you follow a link, the other site's privacy practices apply. We are not responsible for how those sites handle information.

## Your Choices and Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask what personal information we hold about you, to correct it, or to have it deleted. Because we hold very little, most requests concern email correspondence. Send any request to [verdictpoint@gmail.com](mailto:verdictpoint@gmail.com) and we will respond within a reasonable time. You can also limit what is shared by using browser settings or extensions that block third-party requests, such as font downloads.

## Children's Privacy

Verdict Point is written for professionals and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

## Security

The site is served over HTTPS, and our hosting provider applies standard server security. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, so please avoid sending sensitive personal or case information by email.

## Not Legal Advice

Verdict Point publishes information and analysis for general informational purposes only. Contacting us does not create an attorney-client relationship, and we do not provide legal advice or lawyer referrals.

## Changes to This Policy

If the site begins to use new tools, such as analytics or a newsletter, we will update this policy before doing so and change the effective date above.

## Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to [verdictpoint@gmail.com](mailto:verdictpoint@gmail.com).
