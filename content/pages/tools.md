# Legal Tools and Calculators

Free calculators for lawyers, legal staff, and law firm managers. Every tool runs in your browser, shows how it reached its result, and does not send what you enter to Verdict Point.

## Dates and Deadlines

**[Legal Deadline Calculator](/tools/legal-deadline-calculator/)**: Count calendar or business days from a trigger date, skip weekends and federal holidays, and see each step of the count.

## Judgments and Interest

**[Post-Judgment Interest Calculator](/tools/post-judgment-interest-calculator/)**: Calculate interest on a federal civil judgment under 28 U.S.C. 1961, computed daily and compounded annually, with a year-by-year schedule.

## Billing and Time

**[Billable Hours Calculator](/tools/billable-hours-calculator/)**: Convert minutes to billable hours with any increment, plan an annual billable target, and model utilization, realization, and collections.

## Firm Performance

**[Law Firm KPI Calculator](/tools/law-firm-kpi-calculator/)**: Calculate utilization, realization, collection, billing yield, profit margin, revenue per lawyer, and intake costs from your own totals.

## How These Tools Are Built

Each tool explains its formulas or counting rules on the page, lists its sources, and states what it does not do. Calculations run on your device. The tools do not ask for client names, matter details, or any other confidential information.
