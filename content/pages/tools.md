# Legal Tools and Calculators

Free calculators for lawyers, legal staff, and law firm managers. Every tool runs in your browser, shows how it reached its result, and does not send what you enter to Verdict Point.

## How These Tools Are Built

Each tool explains its formulas or counting rules on the page, lists its sources, and states what it does not do. Calculations run on your device. The tools do not ask for client names, matter details, or any other confidential information.
