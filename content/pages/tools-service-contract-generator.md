# Service Contract Generator

A short written services agreement prevents most disputes between clients and freelancers or agencies. This generator drafts one from your choices. Copy it, edit it, and have a lawyer review it before signing.

```tool
service-contract
```

## What the Draft Covers

- **Scope of services:** with changes agreed in writing.
- **Fees:** fixed, hourly, or monthly.
- **Invoicing and payment:** a payment deadline you choose.
- **Term and termination:** with the notice period you choose, and payment for work done.
- **Independent contractor status.**
- **Ownership of work:** the client owns it once paid, or the provider keeps it and licenses it.
- **Confidentiality, warranty, limited liability, and governing law.**

## Choosing Who Owns the Work

Most clients expect to own what they pay for, such as a website, logo, or report. The default draft transfers the deliverables to the client once paid in full, while the provider keeps its pre-existing tools. Some providers prefer to keep ownership and license the work instead, which the second option covers.

## Worked Example

A dental group hires a web studio to build a five-page site for a fixed $6,500. The draft sets 15-day payment terms, 14 days' notice to end, client ownership once paid, and Florida law.

## Limits of the Generator

- **Worker classification** depends on how the work is actually done, not on the label in the contract.
- **Industry rules** may require extra terms, such as licensing or data protection.
- **Not legal advice.** Have a lawyer review the draft.

If you will share sensitive information first, start with the [NDA generator](/tools/nda-generator/). If a client stops paying, the [demand letter generator](/tools/demand-letter-generator/) drafts a payment request.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What Should a Service Contract Include?

At minimum: who the parties are, the services, the price and payment terms, how either side can end it, who owns the work, and which state's law applies.

### Does Calling Someone a Contractor Make Them One?

No. Agencies and courts look at how the work is controlled and done. The contract label alone does not decide it.

### Can I Use This for Ongoing Work?

Yes. Choose a monthly retainer and set a notice period for ending the agreement.
