# Settlement Calculators

These settlement calculators help you organize the numbers in an injury or employment claim. Each one itemizes the figures you enter, applies limits that come from published law where they exist, and shows every step. None of them predicts what a claim is worth.

```toolcards
Fees and Settlements
```

## How Our Settlement Calculators Work

- **Your numbers, itemized.** Losses such as medical bills and lost pay are listed line by line so you can check each one.
- **No fixed multipliers.** We do not multiply bills by a set number to produce a pain and suffering figure. You choose what to test.
- **Published limits only.** Where a statute caps damages, such as the federal caps for Title VII and ADA claims, the calculator cites it and applies it.
- **Net, not just gross.** Fees, costs, and liens are subtracted so you can see what reaches you.

## Which Calculator Should I Use?

- After a crash, start with the [car accident settlement calculator](/tools/car-accident-settlement-calculator/).
- After losing a job, use the [wrongful termination settlement calculator](/tools/wrongful-termination-settlement-calculator/).
- To compare fee arrangements on any recovery, use the [contingency fee calculator](/tools/contingency-fee-calculator/).
