# Dog Bite Settlement Calculator

A dog bite can bring medical bills, scarring, and time off work. This calculator adds up your documented losses, lets you test a pain and suffering figure, and applies Florida's dog bite statute if you choose it. It does not predict a settlement.

```tool
dog-bite
```

## Florida's Dog Bite Statute

Under [section 767.04 of the 2026 Florida Statutes](https://www.flsenate.gov/Laws/Statutes/2026/767.04), a dog owner is liable for bite damages "regardless of the former viciousness of the dog or the owners' knowledge of such viciousness." That applies to bites in a public place, or where the person was lawfully on private property, including the owner's.

The statute also says:

- **Shared fault reduces the award.** Any negligence by the person bitten reduces the owner's liability by that percentage.
- **The "Bad Dog" sign exception.** An owner is not liable if a sign with the words "Bad Dog" was prominently displayed, unless the person bitten was under 6 or the owner's own negligence caused the damages.

The calculator applies both rules when you choose Florida. Most negligence actions must be filed within two years under [section 95.11](https://www.flsenate.gov/Laws/Statutes/2026/95.11).

## Worked Example

A Florida adult is bitten while lawfully on a neighbor's property. They have $6,200 in medical bills, $3,500 in estimated scar treatment, $1,200 in lost wages, and $300 in other costs, for $11,200 in economic losses. They test a $15,000 pain and suffering figure, for a $26,200 claim. A 33% fee, $500 in costs, and a $2,000 lien come out of that.

## Limits of the Calculator

- **Not a prediction.** It does arithmetic on your figures.
- **Other states** may require proof that the owner knew the dog was dangerous.
- **Not legal advice.** Homeowner's or renter's insurance often pays these claims, and a lawyer can review the policy.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is a Dog Owner Always Liable in Florida?

Generally yes, under s. 767.04, regardless of the dog's past behavior. The "Bad Dog" sign exception and the victim's own negligence can reduce or bar recovery.

### Does a "Bad Dog" Sign Protect the Owner?

Only partly. It does not apply if the person bitten was under 6, or if the owner's negligence caused the damages.

### Who Pays a Dog Bite Claim?

Often the owner's homeowner's or renter's insurance. Ask the owner for the policy details.
