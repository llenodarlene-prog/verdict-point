# Law Firm Break-Even Calculator

Every firm has a number of billable hours it must reach each month just to pay its bills. This calculator finds that number from your costs, your hourly rate, and how much of your billed time turns into cash.

```tool
break-even
```

## How the Calculator Works

1. **Monthly overhead:** Add rent, staff, software, other costs, and the pay you need for yourself.
2. **Cash per billed hour:** Multiply your rate by your realization rate (the share of worked time you invoice) and your collection rate (the share of invoices you collect).
3. **Break-even hours:** Divide overhead by the cash per hour.

## Worked Example

A solo firm has $23,000 in monthly costs, including $8,000 in owner pay. It bills $300 an hour, realizes 90% of its time, and collects 92% of invoices. Each billed hour brings in $248.40 in cash. The firm needs about 92.6 billable hours a month, or about 4.4 a day over 21 working days.

## Why Realization and Collection Matter

A firm that bills $300 an hour but writes off time and chases late payments may collect far less. Small changes in these rates move the break-even point a lot. The [law firm KPI calculator](/tools/law-firm-kpi-calculator/) tracks them over time.

## Limits of the Calculator

- **Flat-fee and contingency work** needs a different model. Treat the revenue as monthly cash.
- **Taxes** are not included, so include them in owner pay if needed.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What Is a Good Realization Rate?

It varies by firm and practice. Track your own rate monthly rather than relying on a single benchmark.

### Should Owner Pay Be Included?

Yes. Without it, the break-even point ignores the cost of your own time.

### How Do I Lower My Break-Even Hours?

Cut fixed costs, raise rates, write off less time, or collect faster.
