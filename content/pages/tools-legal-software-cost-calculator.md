# Legal Software Cost Calculator for Law Firms

Enter the number of users, how long you plan to stay, and up to three plans. The calculator shows what each one costs per month, in the first year, and over the whole contract, so a cheap headline price can't hide a large setup fee.

```tool
legal-software-cost
```

## How the Calculator Works

Most legal software is priced per user per month. Many plans also carry firm-wide charges, such as an add-on, a payment account, or a connected accounting product. Some vendors charge a one-time fee for setup, training, or data migration.

The calculator adds those three parts for each option. It spreads the one-time cost across the whole period, so you can compare a plan with no setup fee against one that charges a few thousand dollars up front.

Every price is yours. Copy figures from the vendor's pricing page or, better, from a written quote. The calculator does not look up or store vendor prices.

## Formulas

- **Monthly cost:** price per user × users + flat monthly fees.
- **First-year cost:** monthly cost × 12, or fewer months if the period is shorter, plus one-time setup.
- **Total cost:** monthly cost × months + one-time setup.
- **Cost per user per month:** total cost ÷ months ÷ users.

## Worked Example

A hypothetical five-user firm compares three plans over a 36-month contract. These prices are invented to show the arithmetic.

| Option | Per user | Flat monthly | Setup | Per month | 36-month total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A | $39 | $0 | $0 | $195 | $7,020 |
| B | $49 | $0 | $1,500 | $245 | $10,320 |
| C | $55 | $35 | $500 | $310 | $11,660 |

Option A costs $4,640 less than Option C over three years. Option B looks only $10 a month dearer per user than A, but its setup fee adds $1,500 before anyone logs in.

## What to Include

- **Every paid seat:** lawyers, paralegals, and staff who need their own login.
- **Add-ons you will switch on:** features sold separately, such as accounting, automation, or intake.
- **Connected products:** a separate accounting or payment subscription the plan depends on.
- **One-time work:** setup, migration, and training quotes.

Payment processing fees are left out on purpose, because they depend on how much you collect. Model them with the [payment processing fee calculator](/tools/payment-processing-fee-calculator/).

## Limits of the Model

- Prices stay flat for the whole period. Ask each vendor how renewal pricing works.
- Monthly and yearly billing are treated the same. Enter the rate that matches the term you will sign.
- Taxes, discounts, and promotional rates are not applied unless you enter them in the price.
- The calculator compares cost only. A cheaper plan that lacks a feature you need is not the better buy.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How Do I Compare Legal Software Prices Fairly?

Compare the total over the same number of months, with the same number of users, including every add-on and setup fee. Headline prices per user rarely include those extras.

### Should I Use Monthly or Annual Prices?

Use the price for the billing term you plan to sign. Many vendors charge less per month on a yearly contract, but you commit to the full term.

### Does the Calculator Store My Figures?

No. It runs in your browser and does not send, save, or log what you enter.

The formulas are written out above, and the worked example is checked in the calculator's automated tests. Results that would divide by zero show Not available instead of a number.
