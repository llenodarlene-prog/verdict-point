# Workers Comp Calculator

After a work injury, workers' compensation replaces part of your lost wages while you recover. This calculator applies Florida's statute to estimate those weekly checks. It covers temporary total disability, when you cannot work at all, and temporary partial disability, when you work light duty or fewer hours.

```tool
workers-comp
```

## How Florida Calculates Temporary Disability Benefits

[Section 440.15 of the 2026 Florida Statutes](https://www.flsenate.gov/Laws/Statutes/2026/440.15) sets two weekly rates.

| Benefit | Weekly amount | Limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Temporary total disability | 66.67% of the average weekly wage | Yearly maximum under s. 440.12 |
| Temporary partial disability | 80% of the gap between 80% of the average weekly wage and post-injury earnings | 66.67% of the average weekly wage |

Both benefits share one limit. They stop after 104 weeks combined or when you reach maximum medical improvement, whichever comes first.

## Maximum Weekly Rate and Waiting Period

[Section 440.12](https://www.flsenate.gov/Laws/Statutes/2026/440.12) sets the rules on timing and caps. Compensation is not paid for the first 7 days of disability. If the disability lasts more than 21 days, those first days are paid back from the start.

The weekly maximum equals the statewide average weekly wage for the year of injury. The [Florida Division of Workers' Compensation rate table](https://www.myfloridacfo.com/division/wc/insurer/awwrate/maximum-compensation-rate-table) lists $1,358 for 2026 injuries and $1,295 for 2025 injuries. The minimum is $20 a week.

## Worked Example

A worker injured in 2026 earned an average weekly wage of $1,000. They could not work for 8 weeks, then worked light duty for 12 weeks at $400 a week.

- **Total disability rate:** 66.67% of $1,000 is $666.70. Over 8 weeks, that is $5,333.60.
- **Partial disability rate:** 80% of $1,000 is $800. The gap to $400 is $400, and 80% of that is $320. Over 12 weeks, that is $3,840.
- **Waiting period:** The disability lasted more than 21 days, so the first 7 days are paid.
- **Estimated total:** $9,173.60.

## Limits of the Calculator

- **Florida only, for now.** We add a state only after reading its statute and official rate table directly.
- **Temporary benefits only.** Permanent impairment is paid separately. Use the [impairment rating payout calculator](/tools/impairment-rating-payout-calculator/) for that part.
- **Week-by-week changes.** Partial benefits are compared weekly. If your earnings change from week to week, run the calculator for each stretch.
- **Not a settlement value.** The estimate leaves out medical benefits, settlements, attorney fees, and offsets.
- **Not legal advice.** Confirm your figures with the carrier or a workers' compensation attorney.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How Much Is Workers' Comp in Florida per Week?

Temporary total disability pays 66.67% of your average weekly wage, up to $1,358 a week for 2026 injuries. At a $1,000 average weekly wage, that is $666.70 a week.

### How Long Can I Get Temporary Benefits in Florida?

Up to 104 weeks of temporary total and partial benefits combined, or until you reach maximum medical improvement, whichever comes first.

### Do I Get Paid for the First Week?

Not if the disability lasts 21 days or less. If it lasts longer, Florida pays the first 7 days too.

### What If I Return to Work at Lower Pay?

You may qualify for temporary partial disability. It pays 80% of the difference between 80% of your average weekly wage and what you now earn, up to 66.67% of your wage.
