# Probate Attorney Fee Calculator

Some states set a schedule for probate attorney fees in their statutes. This calculator applies Florida's schedule for ordinary services in a formal estate administration. Enter the inventory value of the probate assets and the income the estate earned, and it shows the fee presumed reasonable, bracket by bracket.

```tool
probate-fee
```

## How Florida Calculates a Probate Attorney Fee

Under [section 733.6171 of the 2026 Florida Statutes](http://www.leg.state.fl.us/statutes/index.cfm?App_mode=Display_Statute&URL=0700-0799/0733/Sections/0733.6171.html), the fee for ordinary services in a formal estate administration is presumed reasonable if it follows a schedule based on the estate's compensable value. Compensable value is the inventory value of the probate estate assets plus the income earned by the estate during administration.

| Compensable value | Fee |
| --- | --- |
| $40,000 or less | $1,500 |
| Over $40,000 up to $70,000 | An additional $750 |
| Over $70,000 up to $100,000 | An additional $750 |
| Next $900,000 (to $1 million) | 3% |
| Over $1 million up to $3 million | 2.5% |
| Over $3 million up to $5 million | 2% |
| Over $5 million up to $10 million | 1.5% |
| Over $10 million | 1% |

If the attorney prepares the federal estate tax return, the statute separately presumes reasonable a fee of 0.5% of the gross estate up to $10 million and 0.25% above that. Enter the gross estate in the optional field to include it.

## The Schedule Is Not Mandatory

The statute requires the attorney to tell the personal representative, before using the schedule, that there is no mandatory statutory attorney fee and that the fee can be negotiated. The parties can agree to a different method, such as hourly billing. The court can also raise or lower the fee on petition, and extraordinary services, such as will contests, tax audits, or real estate sales, are paid on top of the ordinary fee.

## Worked Example

An estate has $850,000 in probate assets and earned $25,000 in income during administration, for a compensable value of $875,000. The first $100,000 produces $3,000 in flat amounts. The next $775,000 at 3% adds $23,250. The ordinary services fee presumed reasonable is $26,250, an effective rate of 3.00%.

## Limits of the Calculator

- **Florida only, for now.** Other states use different schedules or none at all. We add a state only after checking its statute directly. A California mode is planned.
- **Probate assets only.** The statute counts probate estate assets. Assets that pass outside probate, such as jointly owned property, are not part of the value you enter.
- **Ordinary services only.** Extraordinary services are compensated separately and are not included.
- **Not legal advice.** Confirm the fee arrangement in writing with the attorney.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How Much Does a Probate Attorney Charge in Florida?

Florida's statute presumes a fee reasonable when it follows a schedule based on the estate's compensable value: $1,500 up to $40,000, rising in steps and then by percentages above $100,000. Attorneys may also bill hourly or by another agreed method.

### Is the Florida Probate Attorney Fee Mandatory?

No. The statute requires the attorney to disclose that there is no mandatory statutory fee. The schedule is a presumption of reasonableness, not a required charge.

### What Is Compensable Value?

The inventory value of the probate estate assets plus the income earned by the estate during administration.

### Are Extraordinary Services Included?

No. Services such as will contests, tax audits, and real estate sales are compensated separately under the statute.
