# Demand Letter Generator

A demand letter asks someone to pay what they owe before you take formal action. A clear, factual letter often settles the matter. This generator drafts one from your facts and works out the payment deadline.

```tool
demand-letter
```

## What a Good Demand Letter Includes

- **The amount owed** and what it is for.
- **The facts:** dates, what was agreed, and what happened, in a calm tone.
- **A clear deadline.** The generator counts the days from the letter date.
- **How to pay.**
- **What happens next** if they do not pay, such as small claims court.

## Worked Example

A tenant moved out on October 31, 2026, and her $1,850 security deposit was not returned. She dates her letter December 1, 2026, and gives 14 days to pay. The draft sets a deadline of Tuesday, December 15, 2026, and says she will file in small claims court if the deposit is not returned.

## Tips Before You Send

- **Keep it factual.** Avoid insults and threats you would not carry out.
- **Attach copies** of key documents, never the originals.
- **Send it in a way you can prove,** such as certified mail or email with a receipt.
- **Check your deadline to sue.** A demand letter does not pause the limitation period.

## Limits of the Generator

- **No legal claims are added.** The letter states your facts and your request.
- **Collecting someone else's consumer debt** can bring in federal debt collection rules, so ask a lawyer.
- **Not legal advice.**

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is a Demand Letter Required Before Suing?

Not always, but some claims and courts expect one. It also shows you tried to resolve the matter.

### How Long Should I Give Them to Pay?

Ten to 30 days is common. The generator allows 7 to 60 days.

### Should I Mention a Lawyer?

Only if you will really involve one. Choose the next step you plan to take.
