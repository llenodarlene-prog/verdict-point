# Partner With Us

Verdict Point is an independent publication about the business of law. Our readers are managing partners, operations leads, legal marketers, and legal technology buyers. We work with a small number of partners whose material is useful to that audience and can meet the same evidence standards as our own research.

All partnership enquiries are handled by email at [verdictpoint@gmail.com](mailto:verdictpoint@gmail.com). We do not publish a rate card on this page. Terms are agreed in writing for each piece.

## Ways to Work With Us

We consider four kinds of partnership. Each one is reviewed by our editor before anything is scheduled.

- **Sponsored Posts:** A paid article on a topic that fits one of our five sections. Every sponsored post carries a clear sponsorship label, and paid links are marked as sponsored.
- **Guest Posts:** An unpaid, original article from a practitioner or subject specialist. We look for first-hand expertise, named sources, and a topic we have not already covered.
- **Research and Data Collaborations:** A dataset, survey, or benchmark that we can examine and report on. We need the method, sample, and dates before we publish any figure.
- **Source and Correction Suggestions:** A newer source, a missing dataset, or a correction to a published figure. These are always free and are handled as editorial tips.

## What We Accept

A proposal has the best chance when it matches what we already publish. The points below apply to sponsored and guest material alike.

- **Relevant Topics:** Law firm operations, legal technology, legal marketing, and the legal-services market.
- **Verifiable Evidence:** Every statistic needs a public source, its real year, and its sample or scope.
- **Original Writing:** The piece must not be published elsewhere, and the author must hold the rights to it.
- **Named Authors:** We publish the author's real name and role. We do not run anonymous promotional content.

## What We Decline

Some requests do not fit an independent publication, and we would rather say so up front.

- **Undisclosed Paid Placement:** We do not accept payment for links or mentions that are not labeled.
- **Edits to Existing Research for a Fee:** Our published findings change only when the evidence changes.
- **Off-Topic Material:** We decline content about gambling, lending, adult products, or anything unrelated to the business of law.
- **Legal Advice or Client Solicitation:** We do not publish material that offers case-specific guidance or lawyer referrals.

## Editorial Independence

Payment never buys a conclusion. Sponsors do not review or approve our independent articles, and a partnership does not affect how we report on a company or its competitors. We may edit any submitted piece for accuracy, clarity, and house style, and we may decline or remove a piece that does not meet these standards.

## How to Send a Proposal

Email [verdictpoint@gmail.com](mailto:verdictpoint@gmail.com) with the details below so we can reply with a clear answer.

1. **Partnership Type:** Sponsored post, guest post, research collaboration, or source suggestion.
2. **Proposed Topic:** A working title and two or three sentences on what the reader will learn.
3. **Evidence:** Links to the sources or data you plan to use.
4. **Author:** The name, role, and organization of the person who will be credited.
5. **Links:** Any page you would like referenced, and why it helps the reader.

Verdict Point does not provide legal advice, lawyer referrals, or case-specific guidance, and we do not offer phone support.
