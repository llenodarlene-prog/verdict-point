# Legal Billing FAQ: Billable Hours, Fees and Finance

Legal billing is how a law firm turns recorded time and costs into invoices, then into collected fees. These answers cover each step. That runs from logging a six-minute entry to measuring what the firm collects.

## About These Answers

Every answer leads with the direct response, then adds a table, steps, or an example. Sources sit right under the claim they support, and we checked each one against the live page. Invented figures are labeled as examples.

Billing rules can change with the client, the court, or the fee agreement. Treat these answers as general information, not advice on a specific bill.
