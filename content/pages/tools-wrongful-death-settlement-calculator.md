# Wrongful Death Settlement Calculator

A wrongful death claim often turns on the income and support a family lost. This calculator converts years of lost support into today's dollars and adds the other damage types you enter. It does not predict a settlement.

```tool
wrongful-death
```

## Damage Types in Florida

[Section 768.21 of the 2026 Florida Statutes](https://www.flsenate.gov/Laws/Statutes/2026/768.21) lists what survivors and the estate may recover:

- **Lost support and services:** Each survivor may recover the value of lost support and services, with future losses reduced to present value.
- **Surviving spouse:** Loss of companionship and protection, and mental pain and suffering.
- **Minor children:** Lost parental companionship, instruction, and guidance, and mental pain and suffering. All children may recover these if there is no surviving spouse.
- **Parents of a minor child:** Mental pain and suffering.
- **Medical and funeral expenses:** Paid by survivors or charged to the estate.
- **The estate:** Lost earnings from the date of injury to the date of death.

Wrongful death actions generally must be filed within two years under [section 95.11(5)(e)](https://www.flsenate.gov/Laws/Statutes/2026/95.11).

## How Present Value Works

Money received today is worth more than the same amount paid over years. The calculator discounts each year of support by the rate you enter. At 3%, $45,000 a year for 20 years ($900,000 in total) has a present value of about $669,486.

## Worked Example

A family lost $45,000 a year in support for an estimated 20 years. At a 3% discount rate, that is about $669,486 today. Adding $28,000 in medical and funeral costs and a $500,000 figure for companionship and pain and suffering gives a scenario of about $1,197,486.

## Limits of the Calculator

- **Not a prediction.** Every amount is your own input.
- **Discount rates** used by economists and courts vary.
- **Not legal advice.** Wrongful death claims are complex, and a lawyer can explain who may recover.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How Is a Wrongful Death Settlement Calculated?

Claims usually add economic losses, such as lost support, medical bills, and funeral costs, to non-economic losses, such as companionship. Future losses are reduced to present value.

### Who Can Recover in Florida?

Survivors, including a spouse, children, and in some cases parents, plus the estate. Section 768.21 sets out what each may recover.

### How Long Do I Have to File in Florida?

Generally two years, under s. 95.11(5)(e).
