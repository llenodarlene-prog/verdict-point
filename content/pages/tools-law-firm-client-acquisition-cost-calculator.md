# Law Firm Client Acquisition Cost Calculator

Client acquisition cost, or CAC, tells you how much marketing money it takes to sign one new client. This calculator works it out from your spend, leads, and conversion rate, and estimates the budget for a target number of clients.

```tool
marketing-cac
```

## How the Calculator Works

- **Cost per lead** = monthly marketing spend ÷ leads.
- **New clients** = leads × conversion rate.
- **Client acquisition cost** = spend ÷ new clients.
- **Revenue per marketing dollar** = new clients × average fee ÷ spend.
- **Budget for a target** = target clients × current CAC, assuming it holds.

## Worked Example

A firm spends $6,000 a month and gets 80 leads, a cost of $75 per lead. It signs 15% of leads, or 12 clients, so its CAC is $500. With a $4,500 average fee, it brings in $54,000, or $9 for every marketing dollar. To sign 18 clients a month at the same CAC, it would need about $9,000.

## Track It by Channel

A blended CAC can hide one channel that costs far more than the others. Run the calculator separately for search, ads, and referrals. The [law firm SEO ROI calculator](/tools/law-firm-seo-roi-calculator/) focuses on search.

## Limits of the Calculator

- **CAC rarely stays flat** as spending grows.
- **Fee revenue is not profit.** Compare CAC with the margin on each matter.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What Is a Good CAC for a Law Firm?

It depends on your fees and margins. A CAC well below the profit on a typical matter is the goal.

### What Counts as a Lead?

Any inquiry you could follow up, such as a call, form, or chat. Use the same definition every month.

### How Do I Improve Conversion?

Respond faster, qualify leads on the first call, and follow up consistently.
