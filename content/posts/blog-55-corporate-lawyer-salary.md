# Corporate Lawyer Salary by Role and Employer

Corporate lawyer salary figures describe several different jobs. A law-firm associate and an in-house legal department leader need separate comparisons. Their pay sources may also measure different things, including base salary, cash incentives, or equity.

For a concrete employer example, Amazon advertises a [Seattle M&A counsel base range](https://www.amazon.jobs/en/jobs/10529288/corporate-counsel-m-a-legal) of $153,900 to $208,200. A recruiter placement study reports higher cash medians for several legal leadership roles. Those figures describe different positions and compensation measures.

The sources below separate those categories. Use them to identify the closest relevant comparison before assessing an offer or building a hiring budget.

## Key Takeaways

- **Employer Setting:** Separate law-firm associate schedules from positions within a company's own legal department.
- **Role Scope:** Read the responsibilities and reporting line alongside the corporate counsel or senior counsel title.
- **Pay Measure:** An advertised base range differs from a placement study's total cash median.
- **Evidence Period:** The sources below include current job advertisements and historical data with different reference dates.

## Define the Position Before Selecting a Benchmark

The [Bureau of Labor Statistics lawyer profile](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/legal/lawyers.htm) distinguishes law-firm work from corporate counsel employment. It describes in-house counsel as lawyers serving one organization. Their business advice can cover contracts and other legal issues affecting that organization.

For compensation research, identify the position more precisely than corporate lawyer. Use the employer's description to establish whether the work involves transactions, employment, governance, or another specialty.

These distinctions help organize the evidence without inventing a universal career ladder.

| Position Being Compared | Evidence to Seek | Comparison to Avoid |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Law-firm corporate associate | Applicable employer class-year schedule and bonus terms | Treating an in-house leadership median as associate base pay |
| In-house individual contributor | Current job range, responsibilities, and incentive documents | Assuming every counsel title describes the same level |
| Legal department manager | Reporting line, team scope, and role-specific compensation evidence | Ranking the job by title alone |
| General counsel or chief legal officer | Enterprise responsibilities and complete package terms | Describing executive compensation as a general lawyer salary |

This is a research framework, not a rule assigning duties to every title. Titles such as [lawyer vs attorney](/legal/lawyer-vs-attorney/) or counsel say little about scope on their own. A company may organize its legal team differently. Confirm the position's actual scope before selecting comparable jobs.

## Law Firm Associate Pay Uses Its Own Evidence

A named firm's associate schedule can provide a precise base-pay reference. It cannot establish what every corporate practice pays. It also cannot establish the compensation of an in-house lawyer with a similar number of working years.

For example, Ropes & Gray's recruiting disclosure lists $225,000 for its 2025 class. That schedule states a January 1, 2026 effective date. It covers U.S. associate base pay generally, rather than reporting a corporate-specialty median.

The broader [associate salary scale comparison](/law-firms/biglaw-salary-scale/) keeps employer schedules and effective dates separate. Use the applicable class designation when comparing those rates with a firm offer.

Ask which compensation class the firm assigns to a lateral hire. Record any recognized prior-service credit in the written terms. Do not assume that elapsed years since graduation automatically determine the offer's salary row.

Keep counsel and partner offers outside an associate comparison unless the employer supplies matching evidence. A table headed associate base pay does not provide either category's compensation.

## In House Compensation Medians by Role

Major, Lindsey & Africa's [2026 in-house compensation report page](https://info.mlaglobal.com/2026-in-house-counsel-compensation-report) describes 641 accepted placements from 2024 and 2025. Its methodology identifies U.S. positions and dollar amounts. This is recruiter placement evidence, rather than a representative survey of every employed corporate attorney.

The publisher defines total cash as salary plus bonus or a similar short-term incentive. It tracks equity participation without including equity value in these cash totals.

| General counsel or chief legal officer at public companies | $739,000 | 2024–2025 placements |
| General counsel or chief legal officer at private companies | $500,000 | 2024–2025 placements |
| Regional or divisional general counsel | $499,000 | 2024–2025 placements |

These medians describe different placement groups. They do not predict one lawyer's promotion increases. The landing page does not provide a sample count beside each row, which limits subgroup interpretation.

Treat this corporate lawyer salary evidence as role-specific hiring context. Do not label its total cash column base salary. Nor should the report's 2026 title turn its underlying placements into 2026 employment observations.

For a candidate, a closely matching role can inform questions about an offer. For an employer, the same evidence can support further research. Neither use turns a group median into an entitlement or a required pay rate.

## Employer Advertisements Provide More Specific Comparisons

A current employer posting can clarify location, responsibilities, and the pay component being advertised. The examples below come from Amazon's own job pages, checked October 19, 2026. They describe advertised positions, not accepted offers or verified employee earnings.

### An In-House Transactional Role

Amazon's Corporate Counsel M&A Legal posting, job 10529288, covers acquisitions and strategic transactions. It identifies Seattle as the location and lists a $153,900 to $208,200 annual base range.

The posting requires at least five years of post-bar-admission legal experience. It also describes relevant transaction experience. Those requirements make it unsuitable as evidence for a new graduate's starting salary.

Amazon states that this package includes sign-on payments and restricted stock units. The posting does not assign a dollar amount to either component. Keep them separate from the base range rather than estimating a total.

### A Corporate Counsel Title with Different Work

Amazon's Corporate Counsel labor-relations posting, job 10572170, focuses on labor matters. Its responsibilities include investigations, litigation, and work with employee relations and human resources.

That description illustrates why a corporate counsel title does not necessarily identify an M&A position. Match the work before using a title-based comparison.

The same posting supplies these annual base ranges for its listed locations.

| Listed Location | Advertised Annual Base Range | Employer Reference |
| --- | --- | --- |
| New York, NY | $169,300 to $229,000 | Amazon job 10572170 |
| Arlington, VA | $153,900 to $208,200 | Amazon job 10572170 |
| Seattle, WA | $153,900 to $208,200 | Amazon job 10572170 |

These are location-specific ranges within one advertisement. They are not citywide medians or a survey of competing employers. The page says final compensation depends on experience, qualifications, and location.

Before using a posting, confirm that it remains available and retain a dated copy. If the employer removes it, preserve the observation date in your notes. A historical advertisement can document a past range without proving that the employer still offers it.

## A Posted Range Does Not Show the Distribution of Offers

An advertisement supplies endpoints without identifying how many employees or accepted offers fall between them. Its midpoint is arithmetic, not an observed average. Do not label that midpoint the employer's typical salary without additional evidence.

The same limitation applies when comparing two advertisements. Matching endpoints do not establish matching bonuses, responsibilities, or final offers. Keep the job description and package terms attached to each range.

For hiring research, record the number of relevant postings you examined. A small collection of examples should remain a collection of examples. It cannot become a representative market estimate merely because the figures came from employers.

## Separate Salary from Other Compensation

A useful comparison identifies each component and the evidence supporting it. Keep annual base salary, bonus opportunity, and equity terms in separate fields. This prevents an incomplete headline total from obscuring the package's actual structure.

### Base Salary and Cash Incentives

For base salary, record the annual rate and applicable working arrangement. For a bonus, ask whether the quoted amount is a target, guarantee, or actual payment. Obtain the written conditions rather than assuming the advertised opportunity becomes earned income.

Do not subtract an employer's base range from a placement median and call the result a bonus. Those figures describe different groups. Likewise, adding separate median components does not necessarily produce the median of individual total compensation.

The salary-plus-bonus definition in the placement study supplies one consistent cash measure. An employer's offer may present its components differently. Rebuild the comparison using matching definitions before adding totals.

### Equity and One-Time Payments

Ask what type of equity the employer offers and how its documents describe the award. Review vesting, valuation, and any conditions affecting receipt. Keep the employer's stated grant value distinct from cash salary.

For a sign-on payment, read its payment date and any repayment terms. A one-time amount should remain separate from the recurring annual base rate. Otherwise, the first-year comparison can conceal a different ongoing package.

If a component lacks a written value, mark it unresolved. Do not enter zero merely to complete a spreadsheet. Ask the employer for the missing information before relying on the package total.

## Keep Broad Lawyer Wage Data in Context

BLS reports a $159,670 median annual wage for lawyers in May 2025. That is an occupational baseline across lawyer employment. The source does not label it a corporate-specialty median or an in-house counsel salary. The same figure appears among the [legal industry statistics by firm size](/legal/legal-industry-statistics-firm-size/), alongside firm counts and receipts.

A corporate lawyer salary comparison loses precision when it silently borrows that broader figure. Use the BLS number as background, with its actual occupation and year. Seek role-specific evidence for the job being assessed.

The three source types answer distinct questions. An occupational median describes a broad wage distribution. A recruiter placement median describes that recruiter's accepted offers. An employer range describes what a particular advertisement states.

There is no useful reason to average those figures together. Doing so would hide differences in population, timing, and compensation definition. Preserve them as separate evidence instead.

## Build a Comparison You Can Explain

Begin with a written job description and the employer's compensation documents. Then select external evidence that matches the role as closely as possible. Record any mismatch before using the figures in a discussion.

Use this sequence to keep the comparison reproducible.

- **Define the Work:** Identify the specialty, decision responsibilities, reporting line, and any team leadership.
- **Match the Setting:** Separate law firms, company legal departments, and executive leadership roles.
- **Match the Location:** Use the employer's stated office or remote-work compensation arrangement.
- **Match the Pay Measure:** Compare base with base, and consistently defined cash compensation with cash compensation.
- **Preserve the Dates:** Record the source's observation period and the date you checked the page.

For a candidate, these details support specific questions about placement within a range. Explain the responsibilities and qualifications relevant to the employer's requirements. Avoid presenting an unrelated title's median as the answer to your own offer.

For a hiring team, save the reasoning behind the selected comparisons. A similar title is not enough when the responsibilities differ. Document those differences before deciding whether a figure belongs in the comparison.

## Questions to Ask Before Accepting a Package

Resolve the missing terms with the employer rather than interpreting a public benchmark as an employment promise. Ask how the written offer connects its compensation components with the role's responsibilities.

The following questions make that conversation more concrete.

- **Range Placement:** Which factors determine where the offer falls within the advertised base range?
- **Bonus Terms:** What does the target mean, and which written conditions govern eligibility and payment?
- **Equity Terms:** Which award documents govern the grant, vesting, and treatment when employment ends?
- **Review Timing:** When does the next compensation review occur, and what process applies?
- **Additional Payments:** Do sign-on or relocation payments include repayment obligations or other conditions?

Keep the answers alongside the offer and the dated sources. If two documents conflict, request clarification in writing. Do not resolve an employer-specific conflict by choosing whichever public estimate looks more favorable.

The strongest corporate lawyer salary comparison connects the actual job with matching pay evidence. Establish those definitions first, then discuss the amounts and unresolved terms with the employer.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is There One National Salary for Corporate Lawyers?

The sources here do not establish a single national corporate lawyer salary median. They cover different roles, employer settings, and pay measures.

### Is the BLS Lawyer Median an In-House Salary Figure?

No, the cited May 2025 median covers the broader lawyer occupation. It should not be relabeled as an in-house corporate lawyer salary.

### Do the Placement Medians Include Equity Value?

No, the cited MLA cash totals exclude equity value. The publisher tracks whether equity is present separately.

### Is an Advertised Salary Range an Accepted Offer?

No, the employer postings show advertised base ranges. They do not identify a candidate's final corporate lawyer salary.

### Can Corporate Counsel Mean Work Outside Mergers and Acquisitions?

Yes, Amazon's cited corporate counsel posting covers labor relations. Read the responsibilities alongside the title.
