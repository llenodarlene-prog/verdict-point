# Clio vs PracticePanther: Which Fits Your Law Firm Workflow?

In the Clio vs PracticePanther decision, the clearest difference is how much each company tells you before you talk to sales. PracticePanther publishes all four of its plans, from $49 to $114 per user per month on annual billing. Clio shows its Starter plan from $49 per user and asks firms to request pricing for higher tiers.

In any Clio vs PracticePanther comparison, start from the features you need, not the logo. Both are cloud practice management systems that cover matters, time, billing, payments, and documents. The right choice depends on which features you need on day one and how much you value an open price list. This guide uses each vendor's own pages, checked on November 26, 2026, and the site owner's screenshot of Clio's pricing page.

## Key Takeaways

* **PracticePanther prices are public:** Solo $49, Essential $69, Business $89, and Business Pro $114 per user per month billed annually.
* **Clio publishes one entry price:** Its pricing page shows Starter from $49 per user, with AI included on plans from Core up.
* **Trust accounting sits at the top PracticePanther tier:** Business Pro lists trust accounting, operating accounting, and a three-way reconciliation wizard.
* **Intake and e-signature start at PracticePanther Business:** That plan lists intake forms and native e-signature with unlimited sends.
* **Clio highlights support and migration:** Its pricing header offers 24/5 live support, plus training and data migration.

## Billing and Payments

Both products handle time tracking, invoicing, and online payments.

PracticePanther includes time and expense tracking on every plan. Business adds UTBMS coding and LEDES billing. Automated payment reminders can go out before or after an invoice is due. The PracticePanther home page says clients can pay by "credit card, ACH, or eCheck with PantherPayments."

![PracticePanther home page with a 7-day trial offer describing intake, case management, billing, payments, and accounting](/assets/images/posts/clio-vs-practicepanther/practicepanther-screenshot-1600.jpg "PracticePanther home page, captured November 2026.")

On fees, PracticePanther's [Clio comparison page](https://www.practicepanther.com/comparison/clio/) lists PantherPayments at "2.95% for all card transactions, and a 1% fee per eCheck." The same page claims Clio Payments charges 2.95% for standard cards, 3.75% for American Express, and 1% per eCheck. Treat the Clio rates as PracticePanther's claim and confirm them with Clio.

For a broader look at billing-focused tools, see the comparison of [billing software for small law firms](/legal-tech/billing-software-for-small-law-firms/).

## Trust Accounting

Trust accounting is where plan choice matters most.

PracticePanther lists trust accounting on Business Pro, through PantherAccounting Plus. That plan includes "Built-in legal trust accounting, operating accounting, and financial reporting." It also offers a guided wizard to complete three-way trust reconciliations. The features page says the software "can deposit your retainer payments directly into your trust account."

For Clio, our earlier Clio vs MyCase review, based on an owner screenshot from November 20, 2026, found that Clio's Starter plan listed trust and operating account reconciliation. We could not recheck that on Clio's site for this guide, so confirm it in Clio's current plan details.

## Intake and CRM

PracticePanther builds intake into its Business plan. The pricing page says firms can "Create and request unlimited intake forms from your clients for free. No integration needed."

Clio handles intake through a separate product, Clio Grow. The [Clio Grow page](https://www.clio.com/grow/) says it "responds to inquiries, screens for fit and conflicts, books consultations." It can search across all of Clio for conflicts, send fee agreements for e-signature, and collect payment online. Its AI "sends intake forms and follows up on unsigned agreements."

If intake drives your growth, compare the full cost of Clio Manage plus Grow with PracticePanther Business.

## Documents and E-Signature

PracticePanther includes document templates from Solo up. Its features page says you can "upload a template and we'll fill in the contact and matter details for you in one click." Business adds native e-signature with unlimited sends, and the features page says documents can be sent for signature "without the use of third parties."

For Clio, document and signature features vary by plan, and we could not read those details on Clio's site. PracticePanther's comparison page claims Clio relies on a third-party integration with a capped e-signature limit. That is PracticePanther's claim, so ask Clio how signing works on the plan you are considering.

## Support, Migration, and Exit

Clio's pricing header offers 24/5 live support, plus training and data migration.

PracticePanther's pricing page points to training videos, documentation, and a support team. Live trainings scale by plan. Its comparison page says Clio users switching to PracticePanther can get help from a "dedicated data migrations team."

On exit, PracticePanther's pricing page says, "if you ever need to switch to a different platform, you can easily export your data from PracticePanther at any time." Ask Clio for the same commitment in writing. Before you sign either contract, export a test matter and check that documents, notes, time entries, and trust records come out intact.

## A Two-Hour Test Script

Run the same script in both free trials. Each step takes about 20 minutes.

1. **Set up a matter.** Import a contact and open a matter with practice-area fields.
2. **Assign work.** Add a task and a calendar event for another user.
3. **Bill it.** Record time and an expense, then create an invoice.
4. **Collect.** Process a test payment and check where it posts.
5. **Report.** Pull a report of unpaid balances by matter and lawyer.
6. **Exit.** Export the matter and list anything missing.

Score each step as easy, workable, or painful. The product with fewer painful steps on your highest-risk workflow is usually the better fit.

## Firm Profiles: Which Fits Whom

The Clio vs PracticePanther answer changes with firm size and practice type. These profiles use only the published details above.

### Solo Lawyer With Hourly Clients

A solo lawyer who bills hourly and holds little money in trust can start on either entry plan at $49 per user per month. PracticePanther Solo includes document templates and time tracking. Clio Starter, per our earlier owner screenshot, listed trust reconciliation. Run both trials and pick the interface you prefer.

### Small Firm That Holds Client Funds

A firm that takes retainers into trust needs reliable reconciliation. In PracticePanther, that means Business Pro at $114 per user per month billed annually. In Clio, confirm which plan includes the trust features you need and get the price in writing.

### Growth-Focused Firm Running Ads

A firm that spends on marketing needs fast intake and follow-up. PracticePanther Business includes intake forms and e-signature at $89 per user per month billed annually. Clio Grow adds lead tracking, conflict screening, and AI follow-up. Compare the total cost of each path for your team size.

### Firm Billing Insurers

If clients require LEDES invoices, PracticePanther lists LEDES billing on Business. Ask Clio which tier includes LEDES.

## Questions to Settle Before Signing

A Clio vs PracticePanther decision is easier with written answers to these questions:

* **Exact price:** What is the per-user price for the plan with every feature we need?
* **Price lock:** Is that price fixed for the full contract term?
* **Processing rates:** What are the card, American Express, and eCheck rates in our agreement?
* **Migration:** Does data migration cost extra, and what data does it include?
* **Exit:** Can we export all documents, notes, time entries, and trust records at any time?

Save each answer with the date and the name of the person who gave it. Vendor prices and plan contents change often.

## Which Should You Choose?

**Choose PracticePanther if** you want an open price list, built-in intake and e-signature on a mid-tier plan, and trust accounting in the same system on Business Pro.

**Choose Clio if** you value its included support, training, and data migration, want AI on plans from Core up, or need Clio Grow's intake and conflict tools. Get a written quote for the tier you need.

**Run both trials if** you are unsure. Both offer 7-day free trials without a credit card. The Clio vs PracticePanther choice often comes down to which interface your staff will use.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What Is the Main Difference in Clio vs PracticePanther?

Price transparency and plan structure. PracticePanther publishes every tier, while Clio publishes Starter and quotes the rest. Clio sells intake as Clio Grow, while PracticePanther includes intake forms on Business.

### Is Clio or PracticePanther Cheaper?

At entry level, both start at $49 per user per month. PracticePanther publishes all higher tiers, up to $114 annual. Clio quotes higher tiers, so compare written quotes for the features you need.

### Which Has Better Trust Accounting?

PracticePanther lists full trust and operating accounting with a reconciliation wizard on Business Pro. Clio's earlier pricing card listed trust reconciliation on Starter. Confirm current plan details with both vendors.

### Does PracticePanther Have Client Intake?

Yes. Its Business plan includes unlimited intake forms. Clio offers intake through its separate Clio Grow product.

### Can I Switch From Clio to PracticePanther?

PracticePanther says it offers a data migration team for Clio users. Test an export from your current system before you commit.
